Four boosts

One of each per attempt. When a hazard hits, a Shield is spent before Safety Core.

  • Ink Refill

    Fills the meter back up.

    80 coins

  • Shield

    Takes one hit, then 1.25 seconds where nothing can touch you.

    150 coins

  • Slow Time

    Runs the level at 55% speed for 5 seconds.

    200 coins

  • Rocket Start

    Endless only: 60 m down the corridor in 1.5 seconds.

    280 coins

Each copy you hold raises the next one’s price by half, up to three times. You can hold nine of each.

Where gems come from

  • 5 to start with.
  • 4 for every boss you beat, 2 for every hunt, 1 for a flawless clear.
  • 1 for clearing the daily board, 3 for the weekly one.
  • Optional packs of coins and gems in the store, and a one-off purchase that removes ads.
