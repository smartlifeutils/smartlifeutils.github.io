Five pieces of gear

Upgrade them as you play and equip the ones that suit the way you draw.

  • Ink Regulator

    Every line costs less ink.

  • Slow-Dry Formula

    Lines stay longer before they fade.

  • Broad Nib

    Wider lines, so near misses turn into bounces.

  • Rebound Coil

    More lift every time the orb hits your ink.

  • Safety Core

    Takes one hit that would have ended the level.

Four boosts

Bring one of each into a level when you need a hand.

  • Ink Refill

    Fills your ink back up.

  • Shield

    Takes one hit for you.

  • Slow Time

    Slows everything down for a few seconds.

  • Rocket Start

    Blasts an Endless run off to a flying start.

54 orb skins

From simple colours to fire, frost and lava, each with its own glow, trail and bounce. Buy them with coins, or earn the rarest ones with badges and long Endless runs.
