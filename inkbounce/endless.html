Endless

One corridor that keeps getting faster. There is no portal and no finish line: your score is how far the orb gets.

It starts at a speed of 5.3, adds 0.12 every second and tops out at 15, nearly three times as fast, about 81 seconds into a run.

How far did you get?

New hazards join the corridor by distance, and the long runs pay out in badges. Slide to your best run.

Fever

Keep the orb in the air and the combo climbs. Reach ten and the corridor turns gold.

  • Every bounce off your ink counts, up to three per line, and so does every near miss.
  • Let 2.5 seconds pass without one and the combo resets.
  • At ten, Fever opens for 8 seconds: every line costs 30% less ink, and you get 5 coins.
  • Ink orbs in the corridor refill 30% of the meter. Coins are there for the taking too.

Every day has a modifier

The game picks one for the day. Most days are calm; some stretch your ink or push the speed.

Calm Waters

No change. The corridor as it is.

38% of days

Ink Stretch

Your ink goes 25% further.

32% of days

Velocity Surge

Everything runs 8% faster.

30% of days

Challenges and leaderboards

  • Three daily objectives, easy to hard, for 30, 50 and 85 coins. Clear the board for a bonus of at least 50 coins and a gem.
  • Two to four weekly objectives at 100 to 110 coins each, and a board bonus of at least 150 coins and 3 gems.
  • Eleven lifetime milestones. Seven of them hand out epic and legendary orb skins.
  • Rocket Start throws a run 60 m down the corridor in 1.5 seconds, if you fire it in the first 5.

Four leaderboards on Game Center and Google Play Games: best distance, campaign levels cleared, total stars, and the longest run in seconds.
