Endless

One corridor that keeps getting faster. There is no portal and no finish line: your score is how far the orb gets.

Beat your best run, then go after your friends on the leaderboards.

The further, the harder

  • New hazards join the corridor the deeper you go.
  • Ink orbs along the way top up your meter.
  • Every day brings a twist: extra ink, a faster corridor, or a calm run.
  • Long runs earn badges, and the longest ones unlock rare orb skins.

Fever

Keep the orb in the air and your combo climbs. Keep it going and the corridor turns gold.

  • Every bounce and every near miss adds to the combo.
  • Stop bouncing and it starts again from zero.
  • In Fever your ink goes further, and you collect coins.

Challenges and leaderboards

  • New challenges every day and every week, paid in coins and gems.
  • Lifetime milestones that hand out the rarest orb skins.
  • Rocket Start, to blast down the corridor before you have drawn a thing.

Leaderboards on Game Center and Google Play Games for your best distance, your stars and the levels you have cleared.

Free on iPhone and Android

No account and no connection needed to play. It has ads, and one purchase removes them.
