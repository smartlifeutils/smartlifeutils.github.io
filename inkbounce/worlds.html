The boss at level 20

Every world ends with the same stone block, and it takes more hits each time. In Ice Valley it is called The Wall.

  • Its shield stays shut for 6 seconds while it fires tracking arrows at the orb.
  • It warns you for 1.5 seconds, then opens for 3.5.
  • Bounce the orb into the open core to land a hit. The shield only pushes the orb away; the arrows are what end the attempt.
  • Ink never runs out on a boss level, and Fever gives you a shield charge instead of cheaper ink.

It takes 3 hits in Ice Valley and Ancient Grove, 4 in Frost Shrine and Ashen Rift, and 5 in Sun Temple.

Three stars a level

Sixty a world, three hundred in the campaign. Most levels give one for each of these:

  • Reach the portal.
  • Get there with enough ink left, a little over a third of the meter on most levels.
  • Beat the level’s gold par time.

Hunt and boss levels have no ink to save. There the extra stars are for dying at most once, and for not dying at all on your first try. Hunt levels hide the portal until every mini boss is down, and each one goes down in a single hit.
