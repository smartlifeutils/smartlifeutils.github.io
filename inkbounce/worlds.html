The boss at level 20

Every world ends with a stone block that fights back. In Ice Valley it is called The Wall.

  • It hides behind a shield and fires arrows at the orb.
  • When the shield opens, bounce the orb into its core.
  • It cracks with every hit, and each world’s boss takes a few more.
  • You get all the ink you want, so it comes down to timing.

Three stars a level

Three hundred stars in the campaign. On most levels you earn one for each of these:

  • Reach the portal.
  • Save some ink.
  • Be quick about it.

On hunt levels the portal stays hidden until you knock out every mini boss.
