A good report gets fixed fast. Please include:

  • Your device, such as iPhone 13 or Pixel 7
  • The system version, such as iOS 17.4 or Android 14
  • The game version, shown in Settings
  • What happened, and the steps that make it happen again
  • A screenshot or screen recording if you can
