FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The game crashes or won't start. What should I do?

Try these steps in order:

1. Make sure you're on the latest version of Ink Bounce from the store.
2. Fully close and reopen the app.
3. Restart your device.
4. Confirm your device meets the minimum requirements (iOS 15.0+ or Android 7.1+).

If it still crashes, email us with the details listed under Report a bug and we'll investigate.

I lost my progress. Can it be restored?

Your campaign progress, coins and unlocks are stored on your device. If you reinstalled the game, changed devices, or cleared app data, local progress may not carry over.

Ink Bounce does not currently offer cloud save or account sync, so progress is not automatically transferred between devices or restored after clearing app data.

If you believe you lost progress after a purchase, contact us with your device details and approximate play history and we'll do our best to help.

Does Ink Bounce work offline?

Yes — the campaign and endless mode are playable offline. An internet connection is used for ads, some rewards, and analytics. You'll need to be online for those features to work.

How do ads and in-app purchases work?

Ink Bounce is free to play and supported by ads. Optional in-app purchases (for example, coins or removing ads) are available. All purchases are handled securely through the App Store or Google Play.

I bought something but didn't receive it. How do I get a refund or restore it?

First, try restoring purchases from the in-game store screen. If an item still hasn't arrived, contact us with your order/receipt details and we'll help.

Refunds are processed by the store you purchased from:

  • iOS: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android: request a refund through Google Play.
What are the minimum requirements?

Ink Bounce runs on iOS 15.0 or later and Android 7.1 (SDK 25) or later. The game is designed for landscape mobile play.

Report a bug

Found a problem?

Great bug reports help us fix things fast. When you email smartlife.utilities@gmail.com, please include:

  • Device model (e.g. iPhone 13, Pixel 7)
  • Operating system version (e.g. iOS 17.4, Android 14)
  • Game version (shown on the settings screen)
  • A clear description of what happened
  • Steps to reproduce the problem
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if possible
