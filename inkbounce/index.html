One line of ink

The orb launches on its own. You draw what it bounces off, every stroke drains the meter, and each line fades after four seconds.

Draw beneath it

Every line is a surface. The orb leaves it at the angle you drew, so the shape of the stroke is the whole decision.

Reach the portal

The portal never moves. Gravity zones lighten, triple or flip the pull as the orb crosses them, and spikes swing, spin and drop into the gap.

Keep your ink

Lines cost ink while you draw and while you hold. Finish with enough left and the level pays a second star.

Chain a Fever

Ten bounces or near misses in a row, each within 2.5 seconds of the last, open Fever: 8 seconds of ink at 30% off, and 5 coins.

Break the boss

On boss levels the ink never runs out. Fever there gives you a shield charge that takes a hit instead.

Gear you rank up

Five pieces, five ranks each, bought with gems. Two slots to start, a third once you own ten ranks. Switch one on to see it at rank 5.

Ink Regulator spends less ink.

Rank 5: every line costs 15% less in the campaign and 10% less in Endless.

Slow-Dry Formula keeps lines longer.

Rank 5: each line stays 0.75 seconds longer before it fades.

Broad Nib draws a wider line.

Rank 5: lines and the edge the orb hits are 10% wider.

Rebound Coil sends you higher.

Rank 5: 15% more lift off your own ink in the campaign.

Safety Core takes one hit for you.

It blocks one hit that would end the attempt, with 1.2 seconds to recover at rank 5.
