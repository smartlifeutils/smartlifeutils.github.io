How to play

  1. Draw beneath it

    The orb is already moving. Draw a line under it and it bounces off at the angle you drew.

  2. Reach the portal

    Gravity flips, spikes spin and swing. Find the line that gets the orb through.

  3. Mind your ink

    Every stroke uses ink. Save some and the level gives you an extra star.
