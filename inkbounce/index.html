Ink Bounce

Draw. Bounce. Beat the level.

You get one line of ink. Spend it well. The orb is already moving when a level starts — draw beneath it and it bounces, draw carelessly and the portal is still out of reach with nothing left to draw with.

The loop

One line of ink

Simple to hold, hard to spend well. Physics, reflexes, and a meter that does not refill mid-level.

Draw beneath it

The orb is already moving when the level starts. Every line you draw is a surface — the orb leaves it at the angle you gave it, so the shape of the stroke is the whole decision.

Reach the portal

The goal never moves. Everything else does. Gravity inverts, zones rewrite the rules the moment the orb crosses them, and hazards stop being things you avoid and start being things you use.

Don't run dry

Ink is finite, and a wasted line is not a mistake you fix later — it is ink the portal needed. Clearing a level and clearing it clean are two different problems.

At level 20, something is waiting

Five bosses, one at the end of every world. They do not care how fast you are — they punish a lazy line.

Three stars a level. Sixty a world. Three hundred across the campaign.

Endless

One corridor, no finish line

Chain ten and Fever opens

Keep the orb off the ground and the combo climbs. Reach a chain of ten and every line costs 30% less for eight seconds.

The corridor keeps accelerating. Your ink does not get cheaper on its own.

Gear

Five pieces, five ranks each

Permanent upgrades you rank up and equip. Two slots to start with, a third once you have bought ten ranks.

Ink Regulator
Cuts what every line costs.
Slow-Dry Formula
Lines last longer before they fade.
Broad Nib
Draws thicker.
Rebound Coil
Adds lift off your own ink.
Safety Core
Absorbs one hazard.

Ink Refill, Shield and Slow Time go into any attempt. Rocket Start throws an endless run down the corridor before you have drawn a thing.
