Key features

  • The orb is already moving, and it bounces off whatever line you draw.
  • Ink runs out, so every stroke counts.
  • 100 hand-made levels in five worlds, with three stars to win on each.
  • A boss at the end of every world.
  • Endless: one corridor that keeps getting faster, with Fever and leaderboards.
  • Five pieces of gear, four boosts, 54 orb skins and 104 badges, all earned by playing.
  • Plays offline, with no account.
