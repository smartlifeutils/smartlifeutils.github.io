Setting one up

Any period

A week, a month or a year. It starts fresh on its own when the period ends.

More than one category

One budget can cover Groceries and Eating out together.

A heads-up first

You see it coming before you go over, not after.

The free version has three budgets. Premium has as many as you want.

Your track record

See how many months you stayed under budget, and how long your best streak is.

Each budget also shows whether you spent more or less than last time.

Savings goals

Saving for a trip or a new phone? Set a target, add money as you go, and see when you will get there.

  • A reminder at the end of each month
  • A small celebration when you make it

Goals only track progress. The money itself stays in your bank account.
