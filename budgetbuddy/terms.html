A tracking tool, not advice

Budget Buddy is a tool for recording and organising your own spending, income, budgets and savings goals. It does not give financial, tax, legal or investment advice, and nothing in it is a recommendation to make a financial decision.

The app does not connect to your bank. Balances and totals in the app are based only on what you entered and may differ from your bank’s records.

Figures that are estimates

Some figures in the app are estimates worked out from your entries: the balance forecast in Plan ahead, Left to spend today, what is left in a budget, saving plans and pace estimates for goals, subscription suggestions and price-rise notes, and amounts converted to another currency. They depend on the data you entered and on assumptions that may turn out wrong.

Currency rates come from the European Central Bank through frankfurter.dev. They are published once per working day and may differ from the rate your bank or card provider charges. Check figures that matter before you rely on them.
