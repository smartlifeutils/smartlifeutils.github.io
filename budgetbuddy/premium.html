What it costs

Premium renews once a year. The App Store lists it at 9.99 $ and Google Play at 10.99 $ in the US; your store shows the price in your own currency before you pay.

Cancel any time in your store’s subscription settings. On iPhone you can restore a purchase from inside the app, and Family Sharing is supported.
