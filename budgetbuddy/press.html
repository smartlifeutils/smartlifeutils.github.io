What it does

  • Log a purchase or an income in a few taps on its own keypad.
  • Give any category a budget and see what is left and how fast it goes.
  • See the month in charts, and get a recap you can share when it ends.
  • Save toward goals, and keep reminders for the bills that come back.
  • Scan the payment QR code on a bill, and let repeating payments add themselves, with Premium.
  • Keep it all on the phone, locked with a PIN, your face or your fingerprint, with weekly backups to a folder you choose.
