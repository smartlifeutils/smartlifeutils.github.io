What it does

  • Log a purchase or an income in a few taps, and see what the budget has left before you save it.
  • See how much you can still spend today and stay within your budgets.
  • Give any category a budget for a day, week, month or year, with a morning summary and a weekly check-in.
  • Track subscriptions and spot a price rise the moment it lands.
  • See your balance up to 24 months ahead, the month in charts, and a recap you can share when it ends.
  • Save toward goals with a monthly plan, and keep receipt photos and warranty dates with each purchase.
  • Scan the payment QR code on a bill, and let repeating payments add themselves, with Premium.
  • Use it in 26 languages, keep it all on the phone, and lock it with a PIN, your face or your fingerprint.
