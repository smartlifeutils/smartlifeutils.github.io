A keypad for money

Adding a purchase opens on a “How much?” keypad. Type the amount, pick a category or one of your favourites, add a note and a date if you want, and save.

  • Expenses and income each have their own categories and their own donut.
  • Favourite categories get their own chart cards on the Stats tab.
  • Every entry can be edited or deleted from the list later.

Scan the bill Premium

Point the camera at the payment QR code on a bill and the amount, payee and reference fill in. On Android you can also pick a photo or a file of the code.

Budget Buddy remembers the category you chose for each payee, so the next bill from them lands in the same place. It reads:

  • UPN QR (Slovenia), EPC and SEPA codes (Europe), Swiss QR-bills, SPAYD (Czechia) and UPI (India)
  • EMV merchant codes such as PIX (Brazil), PayNow (Singapore) and Swish (Sweden)
  • and any other code with an amount written in its text

What repeats, adds itself

Three tools for the money that comes and goes on a schedule.

Recurring entries Premium

Set rent, salary or a subscription to repeat every day, week, month or year, on the day you choose. It posts itself when you open the app, and one list lets you edit, pause or delete each one.

Category reminders

Ask for a nudge per category, daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, so the purchases you forget get logged. The Reminders screen sorts categories into waiting and on time and shows when you last logged each one.

Your subscription, too

Upgrade to Premium and the app offers to log the subscription as an expense, so it shows up in your month like everything else.

Categories that look like yours

Start with eleven ready-made ones, or make your own with any colour and one of 180 icons, found by typing what it is.

The starters are Bills, Groceries, Car, Shopping, Eating out, Travel and Fun for spending, and Salary, Savings, Investments and Other for income. Free accounts hold up to nine of each kind; Premium has no limit.

Find any purchase

Search ignores accents, so “cafe” finds “Café”. Filter by category, and group the list by day, week, month or year.

Save a filter as a view, with its name, categories, search words and time window, and pin it to the home screen as a card of its own.

Saved views are in 2.4.2, out on Google Play now.

More than one pot of money

Keep personal, group, business and savings money apart. Each account has its own transactions, categories, budgets and recurring entries.

The account overview shows its balance, income and spending, a balance-over-time chart and the list of its monthly recaps. Free covers one account; Premium adds as many as you need.

Bring your history, in your format

Import a CSV from your bank or a spreadsheet in five steps: separator, date format, decimal mark, header row, and which column is the date, amount, category, note and type.

The date format is detected or picked from nine. Missing categories can be created on the way, and you see the totals and sample rows before anything is saved. CSV import is in 2.4.2 on Google Play.
