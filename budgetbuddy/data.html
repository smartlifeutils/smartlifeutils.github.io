Four facts

On the device

Transactions, budgets, goals and settings are stored on your phone only.

No account

Nothing to sign up for and no email address to give. Open it and start.

Data Not Collected

The App Store privacy label, as declared for Budget Buddy.

No data shared

Google Play’s data safety section: no data collected, none shared with third parties.

All of this stays here

Every purchase, budget, chart and recap you see in the app is read from the phone itself.

Lock it

Turn on a PIN, and your phone’s biometrics if you like, and Budget Buddy asks for them every time it opens.

Face ID or Touch ID on iPhone, fingerprint or face recognition on Android, whichever your phone has.

Back it up, weekly

Choose a folder and the app saves a backup there every week. Make one by hand whenever you want, or share it to your cloud drive or your mail.

On a new phone, install Budget Buddy and load the backup. That is also how you move between phones, since there is no sync.

What does use the internet

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play. There is no analytics service in the app.

Premium removes the ads. Your transactions, categories and budgets are never sent anywhere by the app; a backup only leaves the phone when you share it.
