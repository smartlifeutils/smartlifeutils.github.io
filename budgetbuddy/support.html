On the old phone, make a backup and share it to your cloud drive or your mail. Install Budget Buddy on the new phone, open the personal data settings and load that backup.

Your data lives only on the phone, so removing the app removes it. If you had weekly backups on, load the newest file from the folder you chose.

Premium is billed by your store, so you cancel it there: Settings, your name, Subscriptions on iPhone, or Payments and subscriptions in Google Play.

On iPhone, use Restore Purchases on the Premium screen. On Android, sign in to Google Play with the account you bought it with and open the app again.

Check that notifications are allowed for Budget Buddy. On Android, also allow alarms and reminders, and keep the app out of battery saving.
