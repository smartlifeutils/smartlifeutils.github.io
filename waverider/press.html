About SmartLifeUtils

SmartLifeUtils is a one-person studio in Slovenia that makes games and apps for iPhone, iPad and Android: Wave Rider, Ink Bounce and Budget Buddy. See all three

What makes it fun

  • Real water physics: the boat floats, tilts and planes on waves that keep rolling even when you stop
  • Two buttons, throttle and brake, that also flip the boat in the air
  • Boats from a little motor dinghy to a speedboat, a jet ski and a rescue RIB
  • Seas from a calm lake to the Nile, Loch Ness, the open Pacific and the Caribbean
  • Stunts that pay coins: air time, flips, perfect landings and close shaves past rocks
  • Upgrades you feel on the next wave: engine, hull, propeller, trim and fuel
  • Daily missions, a seven-day streak, a badge book and leaderboards
  • Plays offline, with no account needed
