FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The game crashes or won't start. What should I do?

Try these steps in order:

1. Make sure you're on the latest version of Wave Rider from the store.
2. Fully close and reopen the app.
3. Restart your device.
4. Confirm your device meets the minimum requirements (iOS 15.0+ or Android 7.1+).

If it still crashes, email us with the details listed under Report a bug and we'll investigate.

Why won't my boat accelerate when I hold the throttle?

Check the HUD for PROP OUT. The revs climb the moment you touch the throttle, but thrust only arrives when the propeller is actually below the surface. If you're cresting a wave, going over a jump, or riding nose-high, the screw is in the air and the engine is spinning for nothing.

Ease off, let the stern settle back into the water, then open it up again. Upgrading Bite increases how much of the engine reaches the water and how quickly the screw recovers after air.

What does "TURN BACK" mean?

You can't retreat down the course. A rip current builds behind the furthest point you've reached and pushes you forward, and the warning appears when you drift back into it. Turn around and keep going — the current isn't a failure state, just a nudge.

I lost my progress. Can it be restored?

Your coins, pearls, boats, waters, upgrade levels and best distances are stored on your device. If you reinstalled the game, changed devices, or cleared app data, local progress may not carry over.

Wave Rider does not currently offer cloud save or account sync, so progress is not automatically transferred between devices or restored after clearing app data. Leaderboard scores and achievements are the exception — those live with your Google Play Games or Game Center account and follow it to a new device.

If you believe you lost progress after a purchase, contact us with your device details and approximate play history and we'll do our best to help.

Does Wave Rider work offline?

Yes — every boat, water and run is playable offline. An internet connection is used for ads, rewarded-ad rewards, leaderboards and analytics, so you'll need to be online for those features to work.

How do ads and in-app purchases work?

Wave Rider is free to play and supported by ads. Rewarded ads — doubling your coins after a run, or refuelling for a second chance — are always optional; you only see one if you tap to watch it.

Optional in-app purchases include coin and pearl packs, and a one-time Remove Ads purchase that permanently turns off banner and interstitial advertising. Rewarded ads stay available after Remove Ads, because you choose those yourself. All purchases are handled securely through the App Store or Google Play.

I bought something but didn't receive it. How do I get a refund or restore it?

First, try Restore Purchases from the in-game store screen. If an item still hasn't arrived, contact us with your order/receipt details and we'll help.

Note that Remove Ads can be restored on a new device with the same store account, but coin and pearl balances are stored on your device and are not restored.

Refunds are processed by the store you purchased from:

  • iOS: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android: request a refund through Google Play.
How do I change my advertising or privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy Choices in the game to reopen the consent form and change your advertising choices, where that form applies to your region. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device's system settings — see the Privacy Policy for the exact paths on iOS and Android.

What are the minimum requirements?

Wave Rider runs on iOS 15.0 or later and Android 7.1 (SDK 25) or later, on phones and tablets. The game is landscape only.

Report a bug

Found a problem?

Great bug reports help us fix things fast. When you email smartlife.utilities@gmail.com, please include:

  • Device model (e.g. iPhone 13, Pixel 7)
  • Operating system version (e.g. iOS 17.4, Android 14)
  • Game version (shown on the settings screen)
  • Which boat and which water you were on
  • A clear description of what happened
  • Steps to reproduce the problem
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if possible
