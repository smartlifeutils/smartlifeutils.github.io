Big air pays. Flips pay more.

Every stunt pays out in coins, and coins buy upgrades. Riding with style is how you get rich.

  • Airborne · Sent · OrbitAir time pays by the second. Fly higher and the tier multiplies it, up to ×2.5.
  • Flip +200Front or back, same pay. Chain another before you land and it doubles.
  • Perfect landingTouch down with the hull matched to the water. The right angle moves with the wave, so you read the sea, not a timer.
  • Wave hop ×3Clear a whole trough in one flight. Land and launch again fast and the streak multiplies.
  • Clean clearSlip past rocks and reefs without a scrape. The closer the shave, the bigger the pay.
  • The big oneNow and then a rogue wave rolls in. Come out the far side upright for +300.

Get it wrong and you're swimming

Land at the wrong angle and you bury the bow, flip the boat and send the skipper flying. It's funny every time, and it's always your fault. Hit retry and you're back on the water in a second.
