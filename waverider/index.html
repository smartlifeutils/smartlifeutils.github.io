  1. Climb. Hold → AHEAD to build speed up the back of a wave.

  3. Land it. In the air → lifts the nose and ← drops it. Match the water's angle as you touch down.

Big air pays. Flips pay more.

Every stunt pays out in coins, and coins buy upgrades. Riding with style is how you get rich.

  • AirborneAir time pays by the second. Fly higher and the tier climbs from Airborne to Sent to Orbit, multiplying it up to ×2.5.
  • Flip +200Front or back, same pay. Chain another before you land and it doubles.
  • Perfect landingTouch down with the hull matched to the water. The right angle moves with the wave, so it's the sea you have to read.
  • Wave hop ×3Clear a whole trough in one flight. Land and launch again fast and the streak multiplies.
  • Clean clearSlip past rocks and reefs without a scrape. The closer the shave, the bigger the pay.
  • The big one +300Now and then a rogue wave rolls in. Come out the far side upright.
  • WipeoutLand at the wrong angle and you bury the bow and send the skipper flying. It's funny every time, it's always your fault, and retry puts you back on the water in a second.

Something to chase every day

    Badges move you up, from Deckhand to Sea Legend. Leaderboards for best distance, longest air, most flips and total distance.
