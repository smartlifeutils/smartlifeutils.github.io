Five lanes, every boat

All boats tune along the same five lanes, each with 20 levels. Each card shows what the next level actually does.

Engine

Go

More power. You climb the back of a wave faster and reach the crest with more speed.

Hull

Hull

Less drag through the water, and the boat leans further before it goes over.

Propeller

Bite

More grip, and the push comes back sooner when the prop bites again after a landing.

Trim

Angle

More control over the boat's angle, so lining up a landing gets easier.

Fuel

Range

A bigger tank. Fuel cans drift further apart the longer you go, so range is how far a run can reach.

The lanes rename themselves to fit the boat: the dinghy tunes its Outboard and the jet ski its Impeller. Sprint hulls skip the fuel lane altogether, because on a boat that fast more engine is the better way to go further.

What a full upgrade buys

The garage meters for each boat, from 1 to 10. The first number is the boat as it leaves the garage, the second is the same hull with every lane maxed.
